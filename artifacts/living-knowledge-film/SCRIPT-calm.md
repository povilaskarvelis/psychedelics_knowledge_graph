# Living Knowledge — the calm cut (v1)

1920×1080 (rendered 2x and downscaled) · 30 fps · 1:17 · on-screen type, no voiceover · original piano-led score at 90 bpm
(1 beat = 20 frames, 1 bar = 80 frames). Compositions: `Calm` (with music), `CalmSilent`; sources in `src/calm/`.

A new direction next to the full film (SCRIPT.md, v13). Same core idea, told in fewer words and one continuous move:
the paper is science's unit of record; what it reports is buried in prose; AI can extract it and connect it across papers
into a living knowledge graph. The psychedelics knowledge graph is the first one built — an example — and the film
ends on the general vision: every field.

## Director's brief

- **One idea, one move.** The opening is a single camera move: one paper → the field of papers to the horizon → down
  into one page → its pieces lift off → every page gives up its pieces, which rise and connect into the graph above the
  field. No problem catalogue; the picture carries the argument.
- **Fewer words, held longer.** Ten lines in the whole film, each held three seconds or more, each set where the eye
  already is. Colour does the explaining: findings gold, methods rose, context teal — on the page, in the type, in the
  pieces that lift off and in the marks spreading across the field.
- **Calmer energy.** 90 bpm piano and pads, soft percussion, a gentle build as the graph forms, a steady groove under the
  product, an open chord on the end card. No drops, no hits.
- **General first, example second.** Knowledge graphs as an idea for any field; psychedelic science as "the first one we
  built"; "Now imagine this for all of science" to close.
