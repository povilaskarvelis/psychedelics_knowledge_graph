# Product V53

V53 keeps the V52 picture lock and adds a professionally produced instrumental score.

The soundtrack is arranged from royalty-free Apple Loops in the system Chillwave library. It uses an evolving synth and bass foundation, restrained alternating percussion, a short pre-reveal rise, selective textural layers, and two tonal accents. The arrangement follows the film's narrative structure: sparse during the opening problem statement, fuller at the knowledge-graph reveal, quieter through dense analytical scenes, and resolved beneath the product identity.

The final mix is stereo 48 kHz / 24-bit PCM, normalized for social playback to approximately -15.5 LUFS with a -1.2 dB true-peak ceiling. Regenerate it with `./generate-v53-soundtrack.sh`.
