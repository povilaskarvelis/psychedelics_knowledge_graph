# Product V25

V25 reframes the opening as one continuous problem-to-product story:

1. Psychedelic research is growing fast.
2. It spans many scientific domains.
3. Keeping up still means piecing together paper after paper.
4. The papers come together in a living knowledge graph.
5. The graph supports movement from study details to patterns across the field.

The breadth is conveyed inside the motion rather than through another explanatory slide. Papers resolve into the same five-compound, ten-research-area visual language used by the product. Research-area destinations appear in a stagger, receive visible paper traffic, and pulse as the graph forms. The final opening graph carries directly into the drilldown sequence without resetting its structure or headline.
