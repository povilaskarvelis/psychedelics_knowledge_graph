# Product V54

V54 keeps the V52 picture lock and replaces V53's continuous Chillwave loop arrangement with a score composed to the edit.

The new palette is lower and less melodic: dark pad, drone, FX texture, sub bass, and percussion from one coherent Apple Loops production family. No lead or repeating synth riff is used. Long textures are alternated, reversed, pitch-lowered, filtered, and crossfaded so that the underlying sound evolves rather than visibly resetting.

The score has explicit cues at the synthesis bottleneck, knowledge-graph reveal, exploration handoff, four study-detail views, three analytical views, API/MCP scene, live-update entrance, individual paper-ingestion cycles, and outro. Percussion and bass intentionally enter and leave instead of filling the entire film.

The final mix is stereo 48 kHz / 24-bit PCM, mastered to approximately -16.2 LUFS with a -1.3 dB true-peak ceiling. Regenerate it with `./generate-v54-soundtrack.sh`.
