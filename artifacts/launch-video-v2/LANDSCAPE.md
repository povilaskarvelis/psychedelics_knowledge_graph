# Landscape launch film

Output: `dist/psychedelics-knowledge-graph-landscape-v3.mp4` — 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps, 35 seconds, H.264/AAC.

The film is rendered locally with Remotion. No paid API, stock footage, or externally licensed music is used. The soundtrack is synthesized locally. Remotion's free-license eligibility includes individuals, for-profit organizations with up to three employees, and nonprofits; see https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/blob/main/LICENSE.md.

## Story and visual construction

- 0–4 s: Explicit psychedelic research opening, with a paper from the indexed bibliography.
- 4–14 s: Three publications arrange into a simplified compound → topic → source-paper diagram. The duplicate Psilocybin labels converge into one node while the cards and topic labels move to their connected positions. This is an explanatory selection of connections, not an exhaustive reconstruction of the three papers' findings.
- 14–21 s: The site's actual Psilocybin connection SVG is animated and reframed. Its curves, relative edge widths, node sizes, and research-area colors come from the live Analyze view. Labels are enlarged for video.
- 21–28.5 s: Connection curves reshape toward a timeline; bars grow from the real annual publication counts and research-area series. A Safety highlight directs attention. The chart ends in 2025 to avoid visually comparing a partial 2026 with complete years.
- 28.5–35 s: Product name, current counts, address, and concise v2 update.

Exact paper titles, chart counts, and graph geometry are in `src/landscape-data.json`, generated by `capture-landscape.cjs` from the local product and bibliography. Charts depict the indexed corpus, not all published psychedelic research. The graph shows research coverage; it does not assert therapeutic efficacy.

## Reproduce

From this directory:

```sh
node capture-landscape.cjs
node generate-soundtrack.js --landscape
npx remotion render src/index.jsx Landscape dist/psychedelics-knowledge-graph-landscape-v3.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=17 --pixel-format=yuv420p
```

The capture step requires the preview running at http://127.0.0.1:8011/?data-source=local.

The original square compositions and exports remain available.

Format references: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a554265 and https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-videos.
