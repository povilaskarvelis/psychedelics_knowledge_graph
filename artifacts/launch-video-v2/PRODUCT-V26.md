V26 gives each opening idea its own visual beat:

1. **Psychedelic research is growing fast** — the publication timeline and rolling paper count remain on their own.
2. **Across many areas of research** — papers gather around five large, concrete clusters: Targets, Brain, Experience, Clinical, and Real-world use.
3. **But the findings remain scattered across papers** — a shorter line remains on screen for more than three seconds while the individual papers stay visible.
4. **Now it’s all connected in a living knowledge graph** — the five clusters split into the product's ten research-area destinations, the compound list enters, and the graph links form.
5. **From study details to patterns across the field** — the completed graph carries directly into exploration.
