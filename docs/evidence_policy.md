# Evidence Inclusion and Provenance Policy

This policy defines the criteria for including findings in Psychedelics
Knowledge Graph and the information retained to support verification, reuse,
and correction.

## Core principles

1. Each published finding remains traceable to its source report.
2. Findings from primary studies, meta-analyses, and other reviews are stored
   and presented as distinct evidence types.
3. The graph records whether extraction used full text or an abstract alone.
4. Findings are included only when they are supported by the source and can be
   represented accurately.

## Information recorded with each finding

Each finding identifies its source report, the compound or exposure, the related
research concept or outcome, the evidence topic, and the relevant location in
the report. Detailed views may also include study characteristics such as study
design, population, sample size, comparator, dose, time point, outcome measure,
and effect estimate when these are available in the source.
The graph also indicates whether extraction used full text or an abstract alone.

## Evidence types

- **Primary study:** Original empirical research, including clinical trials,
  observational studies, case reports, and preclinical studies. Study design is
  recorded because these designs provide different forms and strengths of
  evidence.
- **Meta-analysis:** A quantitative synthesis of results across studies. Pooled
  estimates and the number of included studies are retained when reported.
- **Review:** A systematic, scoping, umbrella, narrative, or other literature
  review. The graph records the review's main conclusions about relationships
  and the literature it covers.
- **Contextual record:** A protocol, conference abstract, commentary,
  correction, or similar record retained in the searchable record set and audit
  trail. These records are generally excluded from evidence extraction and
  graph relationships.

## Full-text and abstract-only extraction

**Full-text extraction** uses converted article content. Depending on the
report type, extraction may use selected relevant sections rather than the
entire document. **Abstract-only extraction** uses only the report abstract and
is limited to information stated there.

## Validation and graph inclusion

Before publication, extracted findings are checked against their source reports
and terminology is standardized across compounds, conditions, outcomes, and
other evidence topics. Findings that lack adequate source support, require
further review, or would become misleading when simplified are excluded from
the graph.

The bibliography and record audit include reports that do not contribute a
graph relationship. In the overview graph, relationships from primary studies
and reviews require support from at least two reports. Meta-analyses are shown
separately because each meta-analysis already synthesizes multiple studies.

The absence of a relationship from the graph should not be interpreted as
evidence that no relevant research exists.

## Corrections and updates

Corrections are applied to the structured findings derived from the source
report. When a report is reprocessed, superseded findings are replaced or
removed. Each reviewed release updates the graph, bibliography, Methods counts,
and public site together.

project.
