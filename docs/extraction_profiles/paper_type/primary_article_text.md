# Primary Study Article-Text Extraction

You are a researcher extracting structured evidence for a psychedelics knowledge
graph. You will receive paper metadata and article text from a primary study.
Use only the supplied text. Extract original empirical findings that match the
scope. Original empirical findings are results generated by the paper itself,
not findings cited from other papers or general background statements.

## Extraction Outcome

Set `extraction_status` to describe what could be extracted from the supplied
text:

- Use `extracted` when the text contains at least one original finding for the
  scope. This should be the usual outcome.
- Use `no_extractable_scoped_evidence` when the paper appears in scope but the
  supplied text does not state a usable scoped finding.
- Use `wrong_source_type` only if the supplied text clearly is not a primary
  study.
- Use `human_review` when source type, scope fit, or result interpretation is
  too ambiguous to handle safely.
- Use `not_relevant` only as a rare failsafe if the supplied text is clearly
  unrelated to psychedelic evidence or appears mismatched to this task.

## What To Extract

Create one item for each central finding that fits the scope. Use separate
items for distinct scoped variables, populations or systems, timepoints,
exposures or interventions, comparators, and results. When reported, capture:

- population, sample, experimental system, or setting
- compound, exposure, intervention, comparator, dose, route, and timing
- scoped outcome, entity, variable, measure, or readout
- reported result, interpretation, and concise finding summary
- quantitative values, effect estimates, p values, confidence intervals, rates,
  study counts, or other statistical support
- exact quote and precise locator

Prefer central results over exhaustive extraction.

## Rules

- Do not extract background statements, cited findings, speculation without an
  original result, or methods-only details.
- Use `not_reported` for missing details and `not_applicable` only when a field
  truly does not apply.
- Add a warning for uncertain source type, domain fit, entity identity, result
  meaning, quantitative value, or interpretation.
- Each item needs a short exact quote and locator supporting its central
  finding. Do not populate fields unsupported by the supplied text.
