Focus on brain-system evidence: brain-region, circuit, network, connectivity,
neuroimaging, EEG/MEG, PET, oscillation, neural dynamics, or regional
physiology findings.

Prioritize:

- the graph anchor kind: brain region, brain network, neural circuit, or neural
  readout/measure
- named brain regions, named networks, and named circuits separately when the
  text supports the distinction
- modality and measurement type
- neural effect or change, such as increased activation, decreased activation,
  altered connectivity, reduced receptor availability, changed neurotransmitter
  level, or changed sleep architecture
- circuit relationship, such as within-network connectivity, between-network
  connectivity, seed-target coupling, region-to-region coupling, or
  network-subcortical coupling
- population or experimental system
- intervention/exposure, dose or exposure context, comparator, and assessment
  timepoint
- reported neural change and interpretation

Capture analysis method, spatial details, or clinical/behavioral links only
when they are central to the reported brain-system result.

Do not extract ordinary clinical symptom scales in this task unless they are
directly tied to a brain-system endpoint in the reported result.

Do not create standalone clinical, behavioral, subjective-experience, molecular
target, pathway, or pharmacokinetic items here. If no named brain region,
network, or circuit is reported, use a neural readout such as EEG oscillation,
PET signal, activation, connectivity, or neurotransmitter level only when that
readout is itself the central brain-system finding.
