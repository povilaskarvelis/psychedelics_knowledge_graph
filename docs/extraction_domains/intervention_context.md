Focus on intervention-context evidence: psychotherapy, preparation,
integration, set and setting, facilitator or therapist role, dosing-session
structure, music, psychological support, manualized therapy, implementation, or
treatment-model findings.

Prioritize:

- intervention component or context feature
- population and clinical/nonclinical setting
- relationship to outcomes, safety, adherence, or implementation
- session structure and comparator/context when reported

Do not extract ordinary trial methods, blinding, follow-up schedule, or the mere
fact that a drug was administered unless context is a central evidence question.
