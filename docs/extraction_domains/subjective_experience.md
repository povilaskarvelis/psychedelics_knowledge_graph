Focus on Subjective effects evidence: acute subjective effects, altered states,
mystical-type experience, ego dissolution, perceptual effects, phenomenology,
insight, or subjective-experience questionnaires.

Prioritize:

- subjective construct and measure
- broad construct category. Prefer these user-facing construct labels when the
  paper supports them: Mystical-type experience, Ego dissolution, Altered state
  profile, Perceptual alterations, Dissociation,
  Challenging experience, Psychological insight, Emotional breakthrough,
  Connectedness, Empathy, Personal meaning, Spiritual significance, Euphoria,
  Positive affect, Negative affect, Somatic sensations, Subjective intensity,
  Near-death-like experience, or Psychosis-like effects.
- timing relative to dosing/session
- compound, dose, and route
- population, setting, assessment context, and comparator or reference condition
- subjective effect or change, such as increased score, decreased symptom,
  dose-dependent increase, stronger social connection, greater dissociation,
  qualitative transformation, no clear effect, or mixed findings
- relation to clinical or behavioral outcomes only when explicitly analyzed

Capture scale subdimensions, valence/quality, dose-response patterns,
moderators, subgroups, predictors, and therapeutic-process links only when they
are central to the Subjective effects finding. Treat Oceanic Boundlessness as a
subdimension of Mystical-type experience unless the paper explicitly presents it
as a standalone construct.

Use optional controlled context fields when stated: `administration_route`,
`dosing_schedule`, `session_context`, `population_model_category`, and
`study_design_category`. These are coarse filters; keep exact paper wording in
`dose_or_regimen`, `setting_or_context`, `population_or_system`, and
`study_design`.

Do not extract broad consciousness or intoxication discussion unless subjective
experience is measured or centrally reviewed.

Do not create standalone clinical, safety, cognitive/behavioral, brain-system,
molecular, or pharmacokinetic items here. For scale-validation papers, extract
only the central findings needed to represent the subjective-experience
construct and measure; do not turn every validation correlation or subgroup test
into a separate item.

Route persistent or clinically problematic psychosis, mania, depersonalization,
derealization, panic, or distress findings to safety or symptoms when the paper
frames them as adverse events or clinical symptoms. Keep them in Subjective
effects only when the paper measures them as acute phenomenology or subjective
drug effects.

Route creativity-task findings, such as divergent or convergent thinking, to
cognition rather than Subjective effects.
