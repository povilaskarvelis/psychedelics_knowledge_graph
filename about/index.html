About

Psychedelics Knowledge Graph provides a new type of evidence synthesis, enabled by and optimized for the emerging era of agentic science

Why

We are entering the era of agentic science, where AI agents can support many parts of scientific knowledge generation. This creates new possibilities for how scientific evidence is synthesized, organized, and kept up to date. Current evidence synthesis methods such as reviews and meta-analyses are generally bounded in scope, provide uneven coverage across a field, and result in static snapshots that require substantial expert labor to produce and update. Agentic tools make it possible to build living evidence systems that complement these approaches by turning literature discovery, screening, and the organization of findings into a largely automated workflow that can operate at scale. The resulting knowledge graph can be made interactive and explorable, allowing researchers to move between field-level patterns and individual studies, filter the evidence around their interests, and examine relationships across the literature. The evidence base can also be audited, corrected, reused, and extended as the literature evolves.

Current evidence synthesis limitations Opportunity with agentic tools
Continuous synthesis Reviews and meta-analyses are static snapshots of the literature at a particular point in time. Keeping them current as new research appears requires substantial expert labor. Agentic tools can support continuous literature monitoring and updating, allowing the evidence base to evolve as new research appears.
Synthesis at scale Reviews and meta-analyses are usually organized around defined research questions and bodies of literature. As a result, coverage across a broader field can remain uneven. Literature discovery, screening, and the organization of findings can be largely automated, making broader synthesis feasible at scale.
Structured & explorable Reviews and meta-analyses present evidence through a fixed narrative and a predefined set of figures and analyses, limiting how readers can explore the underlying findings. Findings can be organized into an interactive knowledge graph that researchers can filter, explore from multiple perspectives, and navigate from field-level patterns to individual studies.

Psychedelics Knowledge Graph brings this form of living evidence synthesis to the broad and rapidly evolving field of psychedelic science. It converts findings from selected papers into structured evidence that powers an interactive graph and dashboard. Researchers can use these tools to follow emerging trends, identify connections and gaps across the literature, and trace individual findings back to the papers from which they came. AI agents and analytic tools can access public graph data through the REST API or MCP server.

Research community feedback

This is a living research resource, and errors and omissions are expected as the literature grows and the evidence pipeline develops. The project is open source, making its methods and code available for inspection, reuse, and improvement. It is designed to evolve as researchers identify problems, point to missing evidence, and suggest clearer ways to represent the research.
