A. Tonal segmented control

Recommended

A compact switch inside the workspace. The selected mode is communicated by surface depth, stronger text, and a quiet teal tint—not by an exposed edge.

B. Embedded mode bar

The mode switch becomes part of the panel header rather than floating above it. It feels more like changing the entire workspace, but it consumes more vertical space.

C. Minimal mode rail

The lightest treatment: selected text, a soft neutral fill, and a small status light. It blends best with the graph, though it is less emphatic than A or B.

D. Vertical workspace dock

The clearest structural break: modes live on a narrow left rail. It makes Explore and Analyze feel like separate workspaces, but changes the graph’s horizontal framing the most.
